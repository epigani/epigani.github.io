Research appointments
======
* **April 2025–present — Postdoctoral Researcher**, Quantitative Life Sciences, The Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP), Trieste. Research with Jacopo Grilli on the taxonomic and functional organisation of microbial communities, connecting genome statistics, species abundances and metagenomic patterns, and on eco-evolutionary dynamics in fluctuating environments.
* **November 2020–September 2021 — Research Fellow**, Laboratory of Interdisciplinary Physics, University of Padova. Project: Statistical Mechanics of Spatial Ecosystems. PI: Amos Maritan.
* **January–October 2020 — Research Fellow**, ICT Center, Fondazione Bruno Kessler, Trento. Research on opinion dynamics, complex networks and epidemic modelling. PI: Bruno Lepri.

Education
